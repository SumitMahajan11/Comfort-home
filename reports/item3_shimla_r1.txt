=======================================================================================================================================
PHYSICAL MECHANISM [HYPOTHESIS, NOT TESTED]: WHY R1 O2 RISES WHILE R2 AND R3 FALL
=======================================================================================================================================
Status: HYPOTHESIS, NOT TESTED. The following narrative provides a physical hypothesis based on zone energy totals;
envelope-part breakdown (conduction/solar split) is not output by the current simulation model.
1. Baseline Shimla Climate Dynamics:
   - In cold-climate Shimla, Baseline heating demand is dominant for all designs: 141.60 kWh/m2 for R1, 154.29 kWh/m2 for R2, and 87.77 kWh/m2 for R3.
   - However, R1 (Lightweight: uninsulated tin roof, uninsulated single-skin walls, no shading, low thermal mass) already incurs 38.38 kWh/m2 of cooling demand during summer solar peaks, compared to 13.51 kWh/m2 for R2 and only 0.19 kWh/m2 for R3.

2. Climate Warming Impact on R1_Lightweight (Net Rise in O2):
   - At +1.5 C warming, R1 heating energy decreases by 15.64 kWh/m2 (from 141.60 to 125.96 kWh/m2).
   - Concurrently, R1 cooling energy surges by +17.96 kWh/m2 (from 38.38 to 56.34 kWh/m2) due to solar heat transmission through uninsulated lightweight surfaces.
   - Because the cooling increase (+17.96 kWh/m2) exceeds the heating reduction (-15.64 kWh/m2), R1 net total O2 increases by +2.31 kWh/m2 (from 179.99 to 182.30 kWh/m2).
   - At +3.0 C warming, this divergence intensifies: R1 heating drops by 30.21 kWh/m2 (to 111.39 kWh/m2), but cooling surges by +36.72 kWh/m2 (to 75.10 kWh/m2), causing a net O2 increase of +6.51 kWh/m2 (to 186.50 kWh/m2).

3. Climate Warming Impact on R2_Conventional and R3_Code_Compliant (Net Fall in O2):
   - R2 (Conventional: 230 mm brick masonry, heavy thermal mass) possesses high thermal capacitance that buffers daytime solar gains.
   - At +1.5 C, R2 heating drops by 21.12 kWh/m2 (from 154.29 to 133.17 kWh/m2) while cooling rises by only +12.44 kWh/m2 (from 13.51 to 25.95 kWh/m2), yielding a net O2 decrease of -8.68 kWh/m2 (from 167.80 to 159.12 kWh/m2).
   - At +3.0 C, R2 heating drops by 40.51 kWh/m2 while cooling rises by +29.89 kWh/m2, resulting in a net O2 decrease of -10.63 kWh/m2 (from 167.80 to 157.17 kWh/m2).
   - R3 (Code-Compliant: insulated envelope, external overhang shading) blocks solar gain almost entirely.
   - At +1.5 C, R3 heating drops by 8.82 kWh/m2 while cooling increases by only +1.02 kWh/m2 (from 0.19 to 1.21 kWh/m2), yielding a net O2 decrease of -7.80 kWh/m2 (from 87.95 to 80.15 kWh/m2).
   - At +3.0 C, R3 heating drops by 17.22 kWh/m2 while cooling increases by +3.95 kWh/m2, yielding a net O2 decrease of -13.26 kWh/m2 (from 87.95 to 74.69 kWh/m2).
