5.3 RUNTIME & DEFINITION OF O1_ad:
  - Runtime of 556 simulations in tools/run_pass3_morph.py: 291.68 seconds (4.86 minutes).
  - Definition of O1_ad:
    'O1_ad' (also designated 'O1_adapt' in code and CSVs) is the annual thermal discomfort percentage
    computed using the ASHRAE 55 / IMD Adaptive Comfort Model for naturally ventilated (free-running) spaces.
    It evaluates indoor operative temperature against acceptability boundaries (80% and 90% comfort limits)
    derived from the 30-day exponentially weighted running mean outdoor dry-bulb temperature (t_rm).
    In contrast, 'O1_ml' is computed using the ML surrogate champion model (XGBoost/Random Forest)
    incorporating personal metabolic rates and clothing insulation alongside physical environmental variables.
