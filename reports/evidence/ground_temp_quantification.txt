1. DEPTH SELECTION & PHYSICAL RATIONALE:
   - Boundary Condition: Floor surface attached to ground (Outside Boundary Condition = Ground).
   - Depth Chosen: 0.5 m shallow ground temperature from EPW header GROUND TEMPERATURES block.
   - Rationale: Single-zone shelter is a slab-on-grade foundation resting directly on surface soil.
     EnergyPlus documentation recommends shallow ground temperatures (0.5 m) for slab-on-grade floors.
     Deeper layers (2 m, 4 m) reflect deep undisturbed earth suited for basements or geothermal boreholes.
     In Jodhpur, 0.5 m monthly ground temps swing from 18.91 C (Jan) to 34.79 C (Jul).
     The default 18.0 C constant was artificially chilling the slab year-round, turning the floor
     into an infinite heat sink that masked solar and conductive heat gains during 44 C summer heat.

4. PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION & FINDINGS:
   (1) Default 18 C Ground: Unrealistically cool. Operative temperature median is ~23.8 C and max is
       under 27 C for top designs. O1_ml is unnaturally suppressed (mean 4.6%, with several designs < 1.2%),
       while Adaptive 90% penalizes it heavily for COLD discomfort (mean 48.9%).
   (2) EPW 0.5 m Ground: Reflects true seasonal desert soil temperature. Operative temperature rises
       noticeably in summer (mean 95th percentile shifts from ~26.4 C to ~31.1 C, and max shifts from 27.2 C
       to 33.6 C). This proves the floor was indeed acting as an unphysical heat sink in previous versions.
   (3) Adiabatic Floor: Operative temperatures are close to the EPW ground case, confirming that an uncoupled
       floor experiences similar temperature rise without artificial subterranean chilling.
   (4) Impact on O2 (HVAC Energy): Ideal loads energy increases with realistic ground temperatures as cooling
       loads in summer are no longer offset by a permanent 18 C cold floor.
