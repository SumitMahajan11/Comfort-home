4. Explanation of Ground Temperature Proximity & Historical Context:
   a) Scientific Provenance of Ground Temperatures in OneBuilding EPWs:
      - As stated in EPW Line 7 (COMMENTS 2): 'Ground temps represent undisturbed earth temperatures - calculated from this weather data.'
      - OneBuilding calculates undisturbed earth temperatures using the Kusuda-Achenbach annual sinusoidal soil heat diffusion model:
        T(z, t) = T_mean - T_amp * exp(-z * sqrt(pi / (alpha * 365))) * cos(2*pi/365 * (t - t_shift - z/2 * sqrt(365 / (pi * alpha))))
      - Jodhpur (26.26°N, 73.04°E, 218.5 m) and Jaisalmer (26.90°N, 70.92°E, 231.0 m) are neighboring stations in the arid
        Thar Desert biome of Western Rajasthan, separated by only 220 km of open desert terrain with identical soil thermophysical properties.
      - Because their annual mean sol-air temperatures and annual temperature amplitudes are closely matched, their calculated
        ground temperatures are very close (mean difference 0.35 C), but strictly distinct in every monthly value.
   b) Provenance of the 'Identical' Attribution:
      - In the initial codebase prior to Decision D-035 (commits prior to 1ecea7d / 2648cca), ground boundary conditions in the EnergyPlus
        IDF generator were hardcoded to DEFAULT_GROUND_TEMP = 18.0 C across all building surfaces for all cities.
      - Consequently, in historical simulation output files, both Jodhpur and Jaisalmer were simulated with an identical 18.0 C ground
        boundary condition. Decision D-035 replaced this static default with the dynamic EPW ground temperature parser, which now
        correctly assigns the distinct month-by-month temperatures from Line 4.
