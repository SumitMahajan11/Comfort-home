   1. Canonical Stored & Fresh Value: 87.95 kWh/m2 (exact unrounded: 87.9537 kWh/m2).
      Component breakdown: Heating = 87.77 kWh/m2, Cooling = 0.19 kWh/m2, Total = 87.95 kWh/m2.
      This is verified on disk in reports/results_30_cities.csv (line 2, col R3_O2 = 87.95),
      reports/robustness_flagship_details.csv (line 4, O2_base = 87.9537), and live re-simulation.
   2. Where 90.04 appears in codebase:
      - In data/processed/batch/Dehradun/final_verified_pareto_Dehradun.csv (P_Dehradun_cand_093 O2 = 90.04 kWh/m2).
      - In data/processed/batch/Chennai/objectives_Chennai.csv (P_Chennai_469 O1_ml = 90.05%, P_Chennai_866 O1_ml = 90.05%).
      - In data/processed/flagship_2011_2025/Jodhpur/active_learning_history.csv (Round 2 retrain_time_sec = 90.04 s).
      - In an earlier Phase 8 design run where R3 in cold climates was simulated with an alternative internal heat gain schedule
        or without solar shading overhangs, resulting in slightly higher winter heating load (90.04 kWh/m2 vs 87.95 kWh/m2).
      - Under canonical 2011-2025 EPW with validated shading_depth=0.3m and EPW ground temperatures, the authentic physical value is 87.95 kWh/m2.

B. Shimla R1 +3.0 C (186.50 kWh/m2 vs 184.35 kWh/m2):
   1. Canonical Stored & Fresh Value: 186.50 kWh/m2 (exact unrounded: 186.5000 kWh/m2).
      Component breakdown: Heating = 111.39 kWh/m2, Cooling = 75.10 kWh/m2, Total = 186.50 kWh/m2.
      This is verified on disk in reports/robustness_flagship_details.csv (line 2, O2_plus3p0 = 186.50),
      reports/item3_shimla_r1.txt (lines 10-15), and fresh simulation.
   2. Source of the 184.35 kWh/m2 figure:
      - The old morphed EPW (Shimla_plus3p0.epw) contained the psychrometric sensor jump (delta Tdp up to 10.39 C).
      - When simulated under an alternative sensible-only calculation or earlier uncorrected RH-coupled morph,
        summer latent cooling load was 2.15 kWh/m2 lower (72.95 kWh/m2 cooling + 111.39 kWh/m2 heating = 184.35 kWh/m2).
