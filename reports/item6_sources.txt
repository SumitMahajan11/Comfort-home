Question: Does the simulation model allow splitting heating and cooling by envelope part for R2 and R3?
Answer:   THE MODEL CANNOT SPLIT HEATING AND COOLING BY ENVELOPE PART.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technical & Codebase Proof:
  1. EnergyPlus IDF Generator Output Definition (src/sim/render.py: lines 433-439):
     The only energy output variables requested in the rendered EnergyPlus IDF models are:
       - Output:Variable, *, Zone Ideal Loads Zone Total Heating Rate, Hourly;
       - Output:Variable, *, Zone Ideal Loads Zone Total Cooling Rate, Hourly;
     No surface heat conduction variables are requested (e.g., 'Surface Inside Face Conduction Heat Transfer Rate',
     'Zone Opaque Surface Inside Faces Total Conduction Heat Gain Rate', or 'Surface Window Heat Gain Rate').
  2. HVAC System Architecture (ZoneHVAC:IdealLoadsAirSystem):
     The EnergyPlus Ideal Loads Air System operates at the zone thermal balance level. It computes the net sensible and
     latent load required to maintain the zone setpoints (heating setpoint 18.0 °C, cooling setpoint 26.0 °C).
     EnergyPlus reports this as an aggregate zone supply rate; it does not natively allocate what portion of the delivered
     heating or cooling energy was consumed by roof conduction vs wall conduction vs window transmission.
  3. Result Parser Implementation (src/sim/parse.py: lines 80-115):
     The simulation parser extracts sm['total_heating_kwh'] and sm['total_cooling_kwh'] directly from the hourly columns
     'Zone Ideal Loads Zone Total Heating Rate [W]' and 'Zone Ideal Loads Zone Total Cooling Rate [W]'.
     It contains no logic or data fields for component-level surface conduction decomposition.
